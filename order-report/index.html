What you are ordering

What’s in a Tier-3 MSME Due Diligence Report

A full pre-sanction due diligence report for MSME proposals, built from independent portal checks, field visits and third-party confirmations. Every finding is colour-graded and backed by evidence.

Standard Included in every report

KYC of the entity

Company / firm / LLP master data, registration and filings, open charges, PAN, GSTIN, Udyam registration and other statutory identifiers.

KYC of every individual

All directors, partners, proprietors and guarantors — identity and DIN status, with group concerns mapped.

Screening & CERSAI

CERSAI security-interest search, litigation (e-Courts, NCLT, DRT), wilful-defaulter, sanctions, adverse media and statutory dues such as EPFO.

ITR verification

Returns filed, acknowledgements, tax paid and any outstanding demand, for the entity and promoters (with borrower consent).

GST verification

Registration, filing discipline and GSTR-1 / 3B / 2B reconciliation, with notices and e-invoice sample checks.

Sales analysis

Books, ITR, GST and bank statements compared over the years, with customer concentration and debtor ageing.

Optional Added only if your proposal needs it

Residence visit

Geo-tagged visit to the promoters’ residences.

Unit visit

Unannounced visit to the factory, office or godown with stock estimate and neighbour checks.

Security visit

Visit and verification of the property or asset offered as security.

Supplier verification

Registration, references and premises checks of key suppliers, if opted.

Cost vetting

Independent quotations and project-cost checks, if opted.

What you receive

  • A single PDF report with a dashboard and colour grade for each module
  • Geo-tagged evidence from field visits and time-stamped portal records
  • An independent back-check and supervisor sign-off on every case
  • An observations register of points for your sanctioning authority
